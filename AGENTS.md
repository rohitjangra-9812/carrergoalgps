# Strict Anti-Repetition & Uniqueness Guidelines

- **Zero-Tolerance for Repetition**: Never repeat the same question, line, phrase, option, or answer anywhere in the study material, mock test, or resource generation.
- **Unique Content Generation**: Every single item (questions, options, explanations, and short notes) must be entirely distinct, unique, and fresh.
- **Exhaustive Tracking**: Maintain an internal mental log of all previously generated questions and phrases within the current session to ensure absolute diversity across all items. If a concept has already been tested, approach it from a completely different angle or omit it entirely.
